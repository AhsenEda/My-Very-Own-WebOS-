About

Hi, I am Ahsen. I am 15 years old.
This journal tracks reading dates, ratings, and thoughts.
I actually started this project a long time ago but this is a new, organized version of it.
The reason I started this project was to motivate my friend Leyla, who just started writing her own books.
I wanted to be able to write 5-star-reviews to her books even though she hasn't published them (yet).
I hope you enjoy it!
